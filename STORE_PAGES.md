## About

**About NorvexGet**

NorvexGet is a small independent shop for practical everyday accessories —
desk and tech gear, car organization, and home/outdoor lighting. We're a
new store, and we'd rather be upfront about that than pretend otherwise:
every listing is checked by us before it goes live, and we stand behind it
with a 30-day return window and real replies within 24 hours.

We don't chase every trend — we pick a small number of products we'd
actually use ourselves, describe them honestly (including realistic
shipping times), and fix problems fast if something goes wrong. If you have
a question before or after ordering, [Contact us](/pages/contact) — a
person reads every message.

---

## Shipping & Delivery

**Shipping & Delivery**

- **Handling time:** orders are processed within 3 business days of
  purchase.
- **Delivery time:** most orders arrive within **8–18 business days**,
  depending on your location and the item. We ship from our supplier
  network rather than a single local warehouse, which is why delivery
  windows are wider than next-day couriers — we'd rather tell you this
  upfront than promise a date we can't keep.
- **Tracking:** you'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your
  order ships.
- **Delays:** customs, weather, and peak-season volume can occasionally add
  a few extra days. If your order is running late, [contact us](/pages/contact)
  and we'll check the status for you.

---

## Returns & Refunds

**Returns & Refunds**

- You have **30 days** from delivery to request a return or exchange.
- To start a return, [contact us](/pages/contact) with your order number
  and the reason for the return — we'll send you instructions.
- Refunds are issued to your original payment method once the return is
  received (or, for damaged/incorrect items, we may not require the item
  back — handled case by case).
- Items should be unused and in their original packaging where possible.
